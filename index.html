Ergonomics & Mechanics

Why Chair & Rail Height Matters

The 670mm High Stool Advantage: Sitting with your hips higher than your knees opens the femoral triangle, reducing pressure on the femoral vein and allowing blood to return with minimal resistance.

The 370mm Foot Rail / Shelf Elevation: Elevating your feet onto a rail or shelf levels the lower leg, allowing the ankle to pivot freely without requiring knee flexion or grinding surgical hardware.

Standard 450mm Chairs: If practicing on a dining chair, setting the rail to 0mm (floor) or 180mm (step riser) maintains safe joint angles.
